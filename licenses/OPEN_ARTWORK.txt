Open artwork: MusicBrainz / Wikidata / Wikimedia Commons

Only the core CC0 metadata fields from MusicBrainz and structured CC0 Wikidata entity
statements are used. Their open-source server implementations are not redistributed.
Image rights are checked per file using Commons imageinfo/extmetadata before showing it.

Accepted: CC0-1.0, public-domain-mark with Copyrighted=False, CC-BY and CC-BY-SA
2.0, 2.5, 3.0 or 4.0 with creator attribution. Unknown, NC, ND and extra restrictions
are rejected. A free/open platform is not assumed to make every image public domain.

The application preserves author, credit, source description-page URI, licence URI,
first known official album title/date and MusicBrainz release-group URI. It displays a
credit marker beside the image. Press i or click the credit line for full attribution;
Settings / Legal also lists current-session attributions. Original image files are not
modified; display resizing/cropping is declared. CC-BY-SA continues to govern the image
and any applicable adaptation; the application's MIT licence does not relicense artwork.
No fetched third-party album images are included in this source or installer distribution.

Local embedded/folder artwork takes priority. First-album lookup is restricted to the
currently displayed/playing song, with two concurrent jobs, a bounded queue and one-day
positive/negative metadata cache. MusicBrainz starts are spaced by at least 1.1 seconds.
Missing/incomplete metadata, network errors and licence failures return no remote image
and never stop audio. First album means the earliest dated official Album in the matching
recording's available metadata, excluding Compilation/Live/Remix/DJ-mix groups; a soundtrack
can be an original album. It is not a claim of globally complete historical discography.

Official terms, software-source links and implementation checks: open-artwork-services.json.
