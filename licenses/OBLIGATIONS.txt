WheelDesk 1.3 third-party attribution and source notice

The application's original code is MIT. All third-party components retain their upstream terms.
Exact names, versions, original licence expressions, evidence URLs, archive integrity values,
licence-text hashes and obligations are in inventory.json (505 Cargo + 169 npm entries).

Retain this directory with distributions. It contains upstream licence/copyright/NOTICE texts.
Where upstream publishes only a licence declaration, the standard SPDX text is supplied with
its actual package metadata; text_basis records this explicitly. Alternative licence expressions
permit choosing one offered licence; AND expressions require each listed licence.
For MIT/Apache alternatives the MIT option may be used; this does not override additional BSD,
Unicode or other AND terms. No copyleft option is selected when a permissive alternative exists.

MPL-2.0: unchanged exact source archives for every locked MPL package are in sources/.
Recipients may extract them with any tar/gzip tool. See inventory.json source_archive fields.
No modifications to those upstream source files were made. MPL continues to cover those files;
the MIT licence of WheelDesk does not replace it. The source-availability notice and archives
accompany both source and executable distributions. Licence text: standard/MPL-2.0.txt.

(none made to the upstream data). CDLA-Permissive-2.0 data retains its accompanying agreement.
